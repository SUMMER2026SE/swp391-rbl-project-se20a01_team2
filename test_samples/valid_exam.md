# IELTS Reading Practice Test
Duration: 60 minutes
Skill Focus: Reading

## Section 1: Reading Comprehension
The impact of climate change on coastal cities has been widely documented. Sea levels are rising at an unprecedented rate, threatening infrastructure and human settlements.

**Questions:**

1. What is rising at an unprecedented rate?
A) Global temperatures
B) Sea levels
C) Human settlements
D) Infrastructure
Correct: B

2. Which of the following is threatened by the rising sea levels?
A) Mountains
B) Deserts
C) Infrastructure
D) Forests
Correct: C

## Section 2: Vocabulary
Fill in the correct missing word in the following sentences.

**Questions:**
1. The global ________ is causing extreme weather patterns.
A) warming
B) cooling
C) freezing
